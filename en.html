About us

Eitqan Accounting Firm

Eitqan Accounting Firm provides complete accounting and financial solutions for small, medium and large companies. We have been in the market since 2017 and serve more than 200 clients.

Our team brings together experienced accountants and financial advisors with a deep understanding of financial laws and regulations, helping companies stay fully compliant and improve their financial performance.

Our services cover company formation, bookkeeping and financial reporting, audit, tax management and cash-flow planning, with financial advice that supports companies from formation to expansion.

What sets us apart

We combine accounting expertise with a deep understanding of financial laws and regulations, building a correct accounting and document cycle for you from day one.

  • Experienced accountants and financial advisors
  • Full compliance with financial and tax regulations
  • Clear, transparent financial reports at every stage
  • Financial analysis that helps management decide with numbers
